# Organize with Activities

Plans and Records are organized by activity. Dayopt has no to-do list or task manager — an activity shows where the time went instead.

Group activities into a category to tell them apart by color and icon. You can open recent Records for one activity from the calendar whenever you need them.

## Creating an activity

Create an activity from a timeblock's activity picker, or from the sidebar. It only needs a name — color and icon are not set here.

- There's no limit on how many you create. Making fine-grained activities doesn't add to your workload
- Each activity belongs to at most one category. It can't span more than one
- An activity not in any category is treated as uncategorized

## Adding an activity to a timeblock

Each Plan and Record takes exactly one activity. You can't add more than one to the same timeblock.

## Your usual length, shown as a hint

In the activity picker, each name carries its usual length on the right — the median of your recorded lengths over the last four weeks, rounded to 5 minutes. It shows up both in the Inspector's activity picker and in the calendar's create panel.

- An activity with fewer than three records shows nothing
- Tapping an activity in the sidebar to create a timeblock uses this length by default, falling back to the default length in your settings

The line above the date and time in the Inspector when you plan is a different figure. That one is the ratio between what you planned and what you recorded; this one is the median of the records themselves.

## Grouping with categories

A category is a container that groups activities together.

- **Color** — choose from a 10-color palette
- **Icon** — choose from 48 icons organized by use case
- An activity inherits its category's color and icon. Without a category, it shows in a neutral color

Categories have no hierarchy. An activity can belong to only one category.

## View an activity’s recent Records

Choose **Details** from the activity list or a timeblock’s activity menu to see its recent recorded time, median Record length, and Records from the last 30 days. See [View activity details](/docs/activity-details) for how to open a Record in the calendar.

## Archiving

Activities and categories you no longer use can be archived instead of deleted.

- An archived item disappears from the picker. You can't add it to a new timeblock
- Timeblocks that already used it keep their original name and color after archiving
- Archiving a category doesn't archive the activities in it — you just can't add new activities to that category

Archived activities and categories can be restored later.

## Deleting

Deleting for good shows a confirmation screen with the count of Plans and Records it's attached to. Your time records themselves aren't deleted.

- Deleting an activity leaves its timeblocks with no activity attached
- Deleting a category returns its activities to uncategorized
