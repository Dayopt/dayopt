# Record your time

A Record is the time you actually spent — as opposed to a Plan, which is the time you intended to spend. Dayopt keeps these two separate: Plans live in their own lane on the calendar, Records live in another. Nothing becomes a Record automatically; you decide what gets recorded.

## Where a Record comes from

A Record is independent whether you create it directly or copy a Plan. Editing, moving, or deleting it never changes a Plan.

## Turning a Plan into a Record

Once a Plan's time has passed, it stays an **unrecorded Plan** until you do one of the following:

| Method                      | How                                                                                        |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **One tap "Record as-is"**  | Tap the button on the Plan card — it copies the Plan's time range into a new Record        |
| **Drag to the Record lane** | Drag the Plan card into the Record lane; resize it if you want the recorded time to differ |
| **"Confirm this day"**      | Copy ended Plans that do not overlap any existing Record, in one action                    |

## Creating a Record directly

You don't have to start from a Plan. Create a block on the calendar for a time range that has already ended, and Dayopt saves it as a Record automatically — there's no "save as Plan or Record" choice to make. The rule is simple: if the end time is in the future, it's a Plan; if it's now or in the past, it's a Record. The label on the block updates by itself as you adjust the time.

## Comparing Plans and Records

The calendar displays overlapping Plans and Records side by side. Reports compare independent totals for each activity and period: recorded time divided by elapsed planned time. The ratio is unavailable when elapsed planned time is under 15 minutes.

You can freely edit a past Plan's time, title, activity, or note — editing it doesn't turn it into a Record.

## Editing a Record

Click any Record to open the Inspector, where you can adjust:

- **Title, activity, and note** — same fields as a Plan
- **Start / end time** — since a Record reflects something that already happened, you're free to correct it
