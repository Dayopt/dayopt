# Export your data

Everything you have recorded in Dayopt can be written out as a CSV or JSON file. Export is available on every plan, including after a trial or subscription has ended.

## How to export

1. Open **Settings > Data**
2. Under **Export**, choose a format and a range
3. Press **Export** and the file downloads

The range is either your entire history or a period you pick with a start and an end date.

## Choosing a format

| Format   | Use it for               |
| -------- | ------------------------ |
| **JSON** | Backup and restore       |
| **CSV**  | Opening in a spreadsheet |

## What's included

An export contains:

- Your profile
- Timeblocks (both Plans and Records)
- Activity settings
- Calendar settings
- Your other settings
