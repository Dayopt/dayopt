# Pricing

## Price and free trial

Dayopt has one plan at $5 per month. All features are included: plans and records, calendar, weekly, monthly and yearly report views, Google Calendar sync, MCP and API.

Your 45-day free trial starts when you first open the app after signing in. No credit card is required and your trial does not automatically turn into a paid subscription.

## Can I subscribe during my trial?

Yes. Successful payment starts your paid subscription immediately and ends the remaining free trial. An abandoned checkout or failed payment does not end your original trial.

## What happens when access ends?

Creating, editing, syncing and MCP/API use stop. You can still view all your saved calendar and report data, export it, delete it and manage your account. Your saved data is not automatically deleted.

Subscribe in Settings to resume where you left off. Resubscribing does not grant another free trial.

## Cancellation and failed payments

You can cancel in Settings at any time. Access continues until the end of your subscription period. If a renewal payment fails, access continues during Stripe's payment retries while you update your payment method.

See the [refund and cancellation policy](/legal/refund) for refunds.

## Are annual or team plans available?

Only a monthly plan for individuals is currently offered.
