# FAQ: Features

## What is timeboxing?

Timeboxing means dividing your day into fixed blocks of time, each given to one task. Instead of working through a to-do list and hoping to finish, you decide _when_ and _how long_ you will work on each thing. Dayopt is built around this workflow.

## How do I use Dayopt?

Three steps. **Plan** — put your work on the calendar as time blocks. **Track** — record the time you actually spent. **Reflect** — look at the gap between the two. Repeat, and your estimates get closer to reality.

## What is the difference between a plan and a record?

A plan and a record are independent. Dayopt compares time by activity and period. Copying a plan creates a new record and leaves the plan unchanged.

## Can I change plans by drag and drop?

Yes. Drag a block to a new time slot, or drag its bottom edge to change the end time. Plans are meant to be adjusted, not followed perfectly.

## Are there keyboard shortcuts?

Yes. You can create blocks, switch views, move between dates, and search without touching the mouse. Press `Shift+?` in the app to see the full list.

## Is there an activity feature?

Yes. Add an activity to your blocks — "deep work", "meetings", "admin" — and you can see where your time went when you review. Group activities into categories for color and icon coding.

Each block takes **one** activity. Activities are unlimited during your trial and subscription.

## Is there a timer? Can I use it like Pomodoro?

Dayopt is not designed to chase you with a timer. You record what you planned and what you did. The point is rhythm, not pressure.

## What happens to blocks I do not complete?

Plans stay until you delete them, and continue to count as planned time. Deleting a plan does not delete records.

## What can I see when I review?

The Review page shows patterns from your own data:

- Tracked time and your most-used activity
- Time by activity, and where plan and record diverged
- Estimation accuracy per activity
- Records by day of week and by hour
- Blank time — the hours with no name on them

Weekly, monthly and yearly reports are available during your trial and subscription. Saved reports remain readable after access ends.

## Can I import my Google Calendar?

Yes, during your trial or subscription. Dayopt reads your Google Calendar and shows those events alongside your blocks. This is a **one-way import** — Dayopt does not write to Google Calendar.

## Does it work offline?

Basic use works offline, since Dayopt runs as a PWA (Progressive Web App). Changes sync when your connection comes back.

## Does it work across multiple devices?

Yes. Dayopt is a web app, so you can use it from a browser on a computer, tablet, or phone. Your data syncs through the cloud.

## Is there a dark mode?

Yes. Choose light, dark, or follow your system setting.

## Is there an API?

Your trial and subscription include API and MCP access. The MCP server lets an AI assistant read your blocks, and you can subscribe to your schedule from another calendar app through a feed link.

## Can I export my data?

Yes, including after your trial or subscription ends. Your time is yours, and you can take it with you.
